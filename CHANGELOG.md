## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-01

### Added
- **Stained Glass & Tiffany Workshop Engine**: Comprehensive calculation for Glass, Consumables, 8 Labor Stages, Equipment Depreciation, Electricity, Waste Factor, Profit Margin, and VAT.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Full offline-first operation with Service Worker precaching and installation across iOS, Android, and Desktop.
- **PWA Installation Guide Modal**: First-visit onboarding and on-demand device-specific setup instructions.
- **Responsive Mobile Studio Drawer**: Slide-out drawer navigation for touch screens and mobile devices.
- **Printable Quotation Generator**: Formal client certificate with live watermark, QR code verification, studio branding, and clean print styles.
- **Workshop Templates**: Pre-configured templates for Tiffany Lamp Shade, Geometric Terrarium, Stained Glass Rosette, and Sun Catcher.
- **Artisan Calculation Tools**: Solder Estimator (60/40 & 50/50), Perimeter & Foil Estimator, and Circle & Oval Geometry Calculator.
- **Automated CI/CD**: GitHub Actions workflows for continuous integration, GitHub Pages deployment, Dependabot auto-merge, and Google Release Please.
- **Dual Theme Support**: Bespoke Cobalt Atelier dark and light themes.
- **Bilingual Support**: Full Turkish (TR) and English (EN) internationalization.
